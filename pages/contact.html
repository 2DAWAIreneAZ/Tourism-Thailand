Routes by Origin

Find the best way to reach Thailand from your home country

Spain to Thailand

Travel from major Spanish hubs to the heart of Bangkok. Start your journey with Route 1, exploring the capital's history before heading to the vibrant shores of Pattaya.

12–14 Hours Direct/1 Stop Route 1 (Classic)

USA to Thailand

Depart from major U.S. cities for a grand adventure in Northern Thailand. We recommend Route 2 to discover the cultural riches of Chiang Rai and the Golden Triangle.

18–24 Hours Via Asia/Europe Route 2 (Northern)

Argentina to Thailand

A long-haul journey from Buenos Aires leading to the tropical paradise of the Southern Islands. Route 4 is your gateway to Khao Sok and the Gulf islands.

26–30+ Hours 2 Stopovers Route 4 (Southern)

Australia to Thailand

Fast and easy connections from the land down under. Whether you choose the cultural North or the sandy South, Thailand is just a comfortable flight away.

9–12 Hours Direct Flights Route 2 or 4
