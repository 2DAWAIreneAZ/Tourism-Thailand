Routes by Country

From Spain → Route 1

Bangkok – Kanchanaburi – Pattaya & Koh Larn

International Flight Travel from major Spanish cities such as Madrid or Barcelona to Bangkok (Suvarnabhumi Airport – BKK). Flights usually include one stop in Europe or the Middle East.

Estimated total travel time: 12–14 hours, depending on the connection.

Transportation in Thailand Arrive in Bangkok and spend two days exploring the city.
Travel by bus, train, or private transfer to Kanchanaburi (approximately 2–3 hours).
Continue by road to Pattaya (around 2–3 hours).
From Pattaya, take a short ferry to Koh Larn.

Common Airlines Emirates, Qatar Airways, Turkish Airlines, Lufthansa, and Thai Airways.

From the United States → Route 2

Northern Thailand & Golden Triangle

International Flight Depart from major U.S. cities such as Los Angeles, New York, or Dallas to Bangkok, usually with one or two stopovers in Europe or Asia.

Estimated travel time: 18–24 hours.

Transportation in ThailandFrom Bangkok, take a domestic flight to Chiang Rai (about 1.5 hours).
Continue the route by minibus or car to the Golden Triangle, Pai, and Mae Hong Son.
Road travel in the north offers scenic mountain landscapes.

From Argentina → Route 3

Khao Sok – Koh Tao – Koh Phangan

International Flight Travel from Buenos Aires to Bangkok, Phuket, or Krabi, usually with two stopovers. Flying directly to southern Thailand can reduce internal travel time.

Transportation in ThailandFrom Phuket or Krabi, travel by bus or private transfer to Khao Sok National Park (2–3 hours).
Continue to Surat Thani, then take a ferry to Koh Tao and Koh Phangan.

Alternative AirportFlights from Bangkok to Koh Samui are also available and provide easy access to nearby islands.

From Australia → Route 2 or Route 3

Northern Thailand or Southern Islands

International FlightFly from cities such as Sydney, Melbourne, or Brisbane to Bangkok or Phuket, often with direct flights or one short stopover.

Transportation in ThailandFor Route 2 (Northern Thailand): fly domestically from Bangkok to Chiang Rai.
For Route 3 (Southern Thailand): arrive in Phuket or Bangkok and continue by bus and ferry to islands and national parks.
